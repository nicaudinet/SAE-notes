---
title: The dead salmon fMRI study
---
https://prefrontal.org/blog/2009/09/the-story-behind-the-atlantic-salmon/

A satirical study where they put a dead salmon through an fMRI machine and asked it questions about a series of photographs of humans in social situations. The study showed some voxels activating in the salmon brain, *despite the fish being dead*.

This study is used as a cautionary tale against [multiple comparisons](./multiple-comparisons-problem.md) in statistics.
