# Sparse Autoencoders

These are my notes on and surrounding [Sparse Autoencoders](./SAE-sparse-autoencoders.md) (SAEs).

The notes grew out of [my research](https://nicaudinet.com). They mix explainers
written in my own words with summaries of the papers I read along the way.

If you're new to SAEs, this path takes you from the idea to the open problems:

1. [What is an SAE?](./SAE-sparse-autoencoders.md): the architecture, the loss,
   and why we'd expect it to work
2. [Failure modes](./SAE-failure-modes.md): the ways SAEs go wrong, from dead
   neurons to feature absorption
3. [Variants](./sparse-autoencoder-variants.md): TopK, JumpReLU, Matryoshka and
   the other architectures built to fix them
4. [Use cases](./SAE-use-cases.md): what SAEs are actually used for
