> An ideal [SAE](./SAE-sparse-autoencoders.md) decomposes neural activations into interpretable, independently composable units that faithfully represent the internal state of a neural network.

They characterise SAEs by:
- Concept detection: how well do individual features correspond to meaningful concepts
- Interpretability: how human-understandable the features are
- Feature disentanglement: are independent concepts properly separated
- Reconstruction: how faithfully does the SAE reconstruct the model representation

They develop a benchmark for SAE evaluation.

Key requirements:
1) Diversity
2) Extensibility
3) Speed
4) Automation
5) Reproducibility
