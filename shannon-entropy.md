The Shannon $S$ is the average information gained by observing the system. This is calculated from the probability distribution $P$:

$$S[P] = \langle I(p_i) \rangle = \langle \log{\frac{1}{p_i}} \rangle = \sum^n_{i=1} p_i \log{\frac{1}{p_i}}$$

where $0\log{0} = 0$.

The entropy of a system composed of independent parts is the sum of the entropy of the independent parts.
