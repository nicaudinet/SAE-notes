They argue that the combination of sparse decomposition and hierarchical features are not robust, and coin the term [feature absorption](./feature-absorption-in-sparse-autoencoders.md).

They demonstrate a toy model of feature absorption.

They develop an absorption metric to measure this phenomenon.

They test hundreds of SAEs and show that just varying the size or sparsity is not enough.
