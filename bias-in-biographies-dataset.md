- **Link:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/LabHC/bias_in_bios
- **Total Samples:** 396,000

The [Bias in Biographies](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LabHC/bias_in_bios) dataset is a popular dataset of short biographies created by De-Artega et al. in [this paper](./bias-in-bios-a-case-study-of-semantic-representation-bias-in-a-high-stakes-setting.md).

Each sample has three attributes:
- Short textual biography
- Profession label (20 categories)
- Binary Gender label (male/female)

The Ravfogel version of the dataset comes divided into train, test, and dev splits.
