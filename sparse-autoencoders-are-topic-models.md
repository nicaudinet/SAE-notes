They argue that [SAEs](./SAE-sparse-autoencoders.md) can be understood as [topic models](./topic-models.md) by extending [LDA](./latent-dirichlet-allocation.md)  to embedding spaces and showing that the SAE objective is the [MAP](./maximum-a-posteriori-MAP.md) estimator. This implies that features are thematic components rather than steerable directions.
