For a [binary-classifier](./binary-classifier.md),  ==recall== is the fraction of positive samples that were classified correctly:
$$
\text{Recall} = \frac{TP}{TP+FN} = \frac{\text{true positives}}{\text{all positive samples}}
$$
In other words, if by "positive" we mean "relevant":
$$
\text{Recall} = \frac{\text{relevant retrieved instances}}{\text{all relevant instances}}
$$
